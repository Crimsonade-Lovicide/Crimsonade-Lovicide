# 1976 coverage of Miranda's killing

Collected Oct 9. **None of this is a verbatim transcript.** Newspapers.com blocks direct
downloads (HTTP 403), and neither page has a Wayback or archive.today copy. The text below
comes from the pages' OCR as a search index exposes it. Words in quotation marks appeared as
quotations in that index. Everything else is close paraphrase. Before putting any of it on
screen as a quote, check it against the scan (Newspapers.com, Arizona Republic, Publisher
Extra), or against microfilm at the Phoenix Public Library or the Arizona State Library.

## Arizona Republic, Sunday, Feb 1, 1976, p. 1

Clipping: <https://www.newspapers.com/article/arizona-republic-ernesto-miranda/34327790/>

- Headline as OCR'd: **"Ernesto Miranda is killed; subject of landmark case"**
- Miranda, whose conviction the Supreme Court reversed in the 1966 landmark decision, was
  "stabbed to death" Saturday night in Phoenix "in a fight over a barroom card game."
- "Miranda, 34, was stabbed in the chest and abdomen, police said."
- Patrolman Gordon Costa: the attacker was one of two men Miranda had beaten in a fistfight
  minutes earlier at the La Amapola Bar, 231 S. Second St. (the OCR reads "La Amapoia").
- Police believed the killer was an illegal Mexican immigrant and searched downtown for him.
- Minutes after the slaying, police went to the Nogales Bar and arrested a man suspected of
  handing the knife to the killer: Fernando Zamora Rodriguez, 23. He gave police a statement
  and was booked into Maricopa County jail for investigation of murder (Detective Calvin
  Lash).

## Arizona Republic, Thursday, Feb 5, 1976, p. 15

Page: <https://www.newspapers.com/newspage/117868869/>

- Headline: **"Other Missing Alien Charged in Miranda Slaying"**
- An illegal Mexican alien "who police believe fled the city" after the stabbing death of
  Ernesto Miranda was charged Wednesday (Feb 4) with murder in East Phoenix Justice Court
  No. 1. He is Eseziquiel Moreno Perez, 23, also known as Eseziquiel Moreno. A warrant was
  issued after the county attorney's office filed the charge.
- Police had interviewed witnesses who heard Perez make statements about his involvement in
  the death of Miranda, 34, "of Mesa."
- The barmaid, Mary Ann Estrella, said Miranda had beaten Perez and another man. While Miranda
  was out of the room, a man handed Perez a knife and told him to "finish it with this." Perez
  allegedly stabbed Miranda in the chest and abdomen, according to police reports.
- Police took Perez in for questioning the night of the stabbing but released him because
  they had no solid evidence. The next morning he checked out of the hotel where he had been
  staying.
- The names index for the page also lists Fernando Zamora Rodriguez. The headline's
  "Other Missing Alien" implies Rodriguez was also gone by Feb 5. That fits the wire story
  below (held for federal immigration officials), but the page text recovered does not say so.

## Wire story, Feb 1976 (UPI, as run by The New York Times)

Seen only as an excerpt (AncientFaces reprint):

- Perez "was questioned briefly by the police after Mr. Miranda was slain during an argument
  over a card game in a downtown Phoenix bar Saturday night, but he was released."
- Rodriguez, 23, arrested shortly after the slaying, was "held for Federal immigration
  officials after the county attorney's office declined to press charges against him."

## NBC Nightly News, Jan 31, 1976 (Carl Stern)

Read in full earlier. Miranda was stabbed in an argument over less than three dollars at a card
game in a Phoenix bar. One man was arrested that night and "immediately… given the so called
Miranda warnings."
