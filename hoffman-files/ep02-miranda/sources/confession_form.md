# The confession form: source of the on-screen wording

**Source:** *State v. Miranda*, 98 Ariz. 18, 401 P.2d 721 (Ariz. 1965), No. 1394, decided April 22, 1965, opinion by Justice McFarland. The opinion quotes "the signed statement admitted in evidence" in full.

Retrieved Oct 8, 2026 from the Harvard Caselaw Access Project: `https://static.case.law/ariz/98/cases/0018-01.json`

## Typed paragraph at the head of the form (used on screen)

> "I, Ernest A. Miranda, do hereby swear that I make this statement voluntarily and of my own free will, with no threats, coercion, or promises of immunity, and with full knowledge of my legal rights, understanding any statement I make may be used against me."

The U.S. Supreme Court quotes the last clause ("with full knowledge of my legal rights, understanding any statement I make may be used against me") in *Miranda v. Arizona*, 384 U.S. 436 (1966). John Flynn read the whole paragraph to the Court at oral argument on February 28, 1966, giving the name as "Ernesto A. Miranda". The Arizona opinion's quotation of the exhibit has "Ernest A. Miranda", and that is the version the episode uses.

## Closing lines

> "I have read and understand the foregoing statement and hereby swear to its truthfulness. /s/ Ernest A. Miranda. WITNESS: /s/ Carroll Cooley /s/ Wilfred M. Young, #182"

## Related testimony quoted in the opinion

Officer Cooley, asked whether he warned Miranda of his rights: "Yes, Sir, at the heading of the statement is a paragraph typed out, and I read this paragraph to him out-loud." He agreed the statement said nothing about a right to an attorney.

## Not reproduced

The handwritten part of the statement describes the crime and is not shown or quoted in the episode. On screen it is abstracted as blank ruled lines.

## Open conflict

The signed statement gives Miranda's age as 23 ("I, Ernest A. Miranda, am 23 years of age and have completed the 8th grade in school"). Secondary sources give his birth date as March 9, 1941, which would make him 22 on March 13, 1963. The script currently says 22.
