# UNBUILT countdown episode — brief

**Working title:** 10 Soviet Megaprojects That Were Never Built
**Alt titles to A/B:** "Stalin's Moscow That Never Existed: 10 Buildings Ranked" / "The USSR's 10 Most Insane Unbuilt Buildings"
**Why this topic:** Soviet subjects are our only reliable winners: Lenin Short 2,000+, Stalin cathedral Short 827, Tatlin TikTok 1,052 plays, Palace of the Soviets episode 512 views in 2 days (fastest start on the channel).

**Host:** Hugo cold open + transitions between entries ("Number 4…").
**Ranking rule (state it on screen):** ranked by ambition, i.e. how much of the real city it would have changed or replaced.
