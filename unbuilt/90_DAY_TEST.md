## Rules

1. **Budget:** spend the Higgsfield plan's monthly credits only (Ultra, 3,000 a month).
   - **No credit top-ups. No ads or promotions.**
   - Any spend still needs the owner's approval first.
2. **Cadence:** **one long video a week at most**, plus Shorts cut from it, which cost no credits.
   - No parallel sessions publishing several videos a day.
   - Anything already finished and scheduled can still go out.
3. **Effort goes into packaging:** the title, the thumbnail and the first 15 seconds of every video.
4. **Topics:** stick to what has worked so far:
   - Frank Lloyd Wright and other famous architects;
   - real projects that were never built, each with a twist;
   - myth-busting.
5. **Format:** the owner has decided the present format has no future. A low-AI format is being designed (`FORMAT_REPORT_2026-10.md`). Once it's agreed, new videos made during the test use it.
