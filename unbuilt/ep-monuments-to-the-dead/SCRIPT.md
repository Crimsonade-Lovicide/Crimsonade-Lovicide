# UNBUILT Special No. 2: "Monuments to the Dead" (working title)

**Status:** first draft for review, 27 Sep 2026. No shots planned or rendered yet.
**Runtime:** about 7:45. **Format:** 16:9, 720p (1080p upload master). **Host:** Hugo Ashby, on camera and in VO, voice Arthur.
**Release target:** Sat 31 Oct 2026 (Halloween).

**YouTube title (chosen):** *London Nearly Built a Pyramid for 5 Million Dead (and Other Monuments to the Dead)*

**The spine.** Each tomb is bigger than the last: a room, 145 ft, 137 m, 150 m, then a pyramid more than twice the height of St Paul's. Underneath, a second count runs, of how many dead each one would actually have held: 2, then 1, then 1, then **0**, then **5 million**.

**Tone.** It's Halloween, so the look is candlelit and moonlit, with fog and long shadows. The jokes land on committees, money and architects, never on the dead. **No. 3 is played straight:** the story of Eva Perón's body involves real violence, and Peronism is live politics in Argentina.

**Legend**
- `SYNC`: Hugo on camera, lip-synced.
- `VO`: Hugo over b-roll.
- **Pace:** about 2.6 words per second.
- **Confidence tags:** `(M)` marks a figure that is single-sourced or approximate; the wording already hedges it. Everything else is H in `RESEARCH_pyramid.md` / `RESEARCH_others.md`.

## No. 3 · A MONUMENT FOR EVITA · Buenos Aires, 1952 (3:10–4:25)

*Played straight. Hugo's dryness goes to the scale only.*

> She was meant to lie beneath one of the largest monuments on earth.

**[P6] SYNC** · Hugo at the cemetery gates, quiet

**[Y1] SYNC** · the summit of Primrose Hill at dusk, the London skyline and St Paul's in the distance

**[Y2] VO** · 1820s London: crowded churchyards, graves re-dug, coffins stacked in vaults by lamplight

**[Y3] VO** · the pyramid rising over Regent's Park: stepped brick faced with granite, an obelisk and observatory at the summit

**[Y5] VO** · Willson's prospectus, the engraving, numbers circled in ink
> It would cost two and a half million pounds and make about fifteen million in profit. And its eighteen acres, stacked ninety-four storeys high, would "generate nearly 1000 acres, self-created out of the void space overhead."

**[Y7] VO** · the Literary Gazette page; then gentlemen laughing in a coffee house
