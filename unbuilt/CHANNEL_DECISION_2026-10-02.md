# Is UNBUILT worth keeping? (2 Oct 2026)

**Short answer:** the topic area has a proven audience. This channel has not yet shown it can reach that audience. On today's numbers, each episode costs 10–40 times what it could earn even once the channel is monetised. It's too early (17 days) to call it dead, but too early to justify the current spending. **Recommendation:** keep it for one capped 90-day test with pre-agreed stop rules, at a fraction of the current spend. Stop if the test fails.

## The economics

- **Partner Programme thresholds:** 1,000 subscribers, plus either 4,000 public long-form watch hours in 12 months or 10M Shorts views in 90 days.
  - At about 1 subscriber a day, reaching 1,000 takes **about 2.5 years**.
  - At about 1.5–4.5 watch hours a day, 4,000 hours takes **years**.
  - Channels don't grow in a straight line, so treat these as what happens if nothing breaks out, not as forecasts.
- **Cost against earnings:** an episode costs roughly 600–1,000 credits, about $20–50.
  - At an ad revenue rate in the single dollars per 1,000 views (an assumption; it varies a lot by audience and country), a long video needs **about 10,000–40,000 views just to cover its credits**.
  - Today's long videos get 20–900 views in their first two weeks.
- **Implication:** the channel only pays if one of the videos breaks out. On these numbers, the money question is "how likely is a breakout, and how much does each attempt cost", not "can it grind its way there".

## What's working

- **The audience exists.** The B1M and Megaprojects are large channels in this space, and one Sun Gun Short elsewhere has 16.1M views.
- **YouTube has tested us, and people clicked.** The Mile-High (902) and Ellis Island (707) got home-feed waves with no ads behind them.
- **Retention is respectable on the hosted formats:** 39–42% watched on Hugo's special and on Chiara's Disney episode.
- **The research is better than in most AI history channels.** The fact-checking is a real point of difference, and it's what YouTube's originality rules reward.

## What isn't

- **Viewers don't turn into subscribers:** about 1 per 450 views. That points to a channel identity viewers don't come back for.
- **Cold viewers leave:** Hugo's home-feed viewers left within about 3 seconds.
- **Output has run ahead of the evidence:** 32 videos in 17 days, from several sessions working in parallel.

## The policy risk (material)

**Fact:** in July 2025, YouTube renamed its "repetitious content" rule to "inauthentic content", targeting mass-produced, repetitive videos. Channels that are mostly AI visuals with synthetic narration, posted at high volume, are the ones that get reviewed.

**Unknown:** how a reviewer would judge this channel. Original research and a consistent host help. High volume, templated formats and synthetic voices hurt. **Hitting the thresholds would not guarantee being accepted into the Partner Programme.**

## Recommendation: a 90-day capped test, 5 Oct 2026 to 5 Jan 2027

1. **Budget cap:** the plan's monthly credits only. No top-ups, no ads.
2. **Fewer, better videos:**
   - One long video a week, with Shorts cut from it (they cost nothing).
   - Stop running sessions in parallel to publish several videos a day.
3. **Put the effort into packaging**, meaning titles, thumbnails and the first 15 seconds. That's where the cold viewers are being lost.
4. **Stick to what has worked:** Frank Lloyd Wright and famous architects, real-but-unbuilt projects with a twist, myth-busting.
5. **Stop rules (my judgement, set in advance so the result can't be argued away). Continue past 5 Jan only if at least two of these are met:**
   - 400 or more subscribers in total.
   - At least one video above 10,000 organic views.
   - A home-feed click-through rate of 4% or more in Studio.
   - 3 or more subscribers per 1,000 views.
   - **If fewer than two are met, stop.** Leave the channel and the videos up (keeping them costs nothing), and stop spending.

## What would change this assessment

- **Earlier:** a video breaking 10,000 views organically.
- **Against:** the Monday data showing Wardenclyffe's roughly 780 new views came from ad traffic rather than the home feed.
- **Against, if the answer is yes:** whether your time has a better-paying use, which is a question only you can answer.
