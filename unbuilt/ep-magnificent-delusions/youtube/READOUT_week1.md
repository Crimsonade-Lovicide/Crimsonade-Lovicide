## Recommendations for episode 2

1. **Get to the first payoff inside 10 seconds.** The Halloween special opens with a 4-second sting before Hugo speaks. Trim it to about 1.5 s, or move it after his first line. This costs nothing, because the special isn't uploaded yet.
2. **Put the most gripping subject at No. 5, and open it on its best image.** In the special that is Washington's empty tomb, with the line "He isn't in it". Cut any setup before it.
3. **Name a searchable subject in the title.** Search brought the channel 575 views; the largest share, 209, went to "London Tried to Beat the Eiffel Tower". Episode 1 got none. Put a proper name in each Short's title too.
4. **Set Related video on every Short.** It is the only route from the Shorts feed, the channel's biggest source, to the long videos, and it has sent 3 views in two weeks.
5. **Read click-through and the Test & Compare result in Studio before 10 Oct**, and use them to pick the special's lead thumbnail.
