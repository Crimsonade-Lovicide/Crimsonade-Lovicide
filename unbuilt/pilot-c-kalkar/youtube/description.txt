West Germany spent around seven billion marks on a fast breeder reactor at Kalkar, on the Lower Rhine. It was finished in 1985 and kept hot and ready for six years. It never produced a single watt. Today there's a swing ride inside its cooling tower.

This is the story of the farmer next door who fought it for thirteen years and gave the Constitutional Court one of its landmark cases, the state government that never signed the last two permits, the 205 plutonium fuel elements that were never loaded, and the Dutch amusement-park owner who bought the whole site for a few million.
