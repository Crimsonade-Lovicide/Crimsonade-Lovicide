What if Germany had won the Second World War? We don't have to imagine it. The regime's own plans survive: the minutes that expected "tens of millions" to starve, the General Plan for the East, the Wannsee list that counted the Jews of England and Ireland, the SS arrest list for Britain with Churchill on it, and the model of the capital Hitler wanted finished by 1950.

This is UNHISTORY, a new series from UNBUILT. We change one real turning point, May 1940, when Lord Halifax could have become prime minister. We mark every stretch on screen: HISTORY for what really happened, UNHISTORY for our timeline. And we stop where the evidence stops.
