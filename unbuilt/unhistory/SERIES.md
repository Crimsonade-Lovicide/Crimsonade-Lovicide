# UNHISTORY: series guide (set 14 Oct 2026)

**Decided by the owner on 14 Oct 2026:**
- The name is UNHISTORY.
- It runs as a named series on the UNBUILT channel first, not as a new channel.
- Real photos only.

Also read `../WORKING_AGREEMENT.md` and `../90_DAY_TEST.md`. UNHISTORY episodes count toward UNBUILT's one long video a week.

## What it is

Each episode answers one question: what would the world look like if one major event had gone the other way?

Most "what if" channels make the alternate world up. UNHISTORY builds it from the evidence: documents, plans and data that really existed. Where the evidence runs out, the narrator says so.

## The four rules

1. **Change as little as possible.** Pick one turning point that really could have gone the other way, with a documented reason. Historians call this the "minimal rewrite rule". Examples: a meeting that ended differently, or a decision that was argued over at the time.
2. **Label every claim on screen** with one of three tags:
   - **HAPPENED**: real and sourced. It is rated H in the episode's RESEARCH.md.
   - **LIKELY**: what historians mostly expect would follow. It has a source, and the script hedges it.
   - **GUESS**: our reasoning, with no source that settles it. The script says it's a guess.
3. **Admit where it stops.** Confidence falls fast with time. Each episode sets an end year, and past it the narrator says "from here, nobody knows."
4. **Real pictures only.** The real timeline gets archival photos. The alternate world appears only through:
   - real plans and documents, and photos of them;
   - charts and maps drawn by the toolkit, such as a dashed line splitting from the real one.

   There are no AI images, and no AI generations of any kind (the owner confirmed this on 14 Oct, after asking whether Higgsfield shots would help; see the reasoning in the episode 1 notes). The owner narrates in their own voice, so uploads can set "Altered or synthetic content: No".

## The look

- The same toolkit and paper palette as UNBUILT, with one addition: the HAPPENED / LIKELY / GUESS tag in the lower-left corner whenever a claim is on screen.
- The title card reads "UNHISTORY", with "an UNBUILT series" under it.
- Titles start with "What If…", because that's what people search for. Each title also carries the episode's own angle, so it doesn't copy the many "what if" videos already out there.

## Subjects about war, genocide or disaster

- **The context goes in the video itself.** It has to be in the narration and on screen, not only in the description: YouTube judges educational context from the images and audio. Source: YouTube's violent or graphic content and hate speech policies.
- **No swastikas or other extremist symbols in thumbnails.** Avoid them in the edit too, unless a document itself carries one and it is shown for a reason. Thumbnails fall under the same policies.
- **The victims are never the joke,** and the tone is never "cool". Plans for mass murder are described plainly, from the documents.

## Before each episode: research and stop rules

Each episode needs:
- a RESEARCH.md with H/M/L ratings, as in the pilots;
- a check of existing "what if" videos on the same subject, so the angle is new;
- a stop rule agreed in advance: what result would mean the series should change or stop. See the episode OUTLINE.md.
