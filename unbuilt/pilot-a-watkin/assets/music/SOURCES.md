**Why this one:** it holds a steady level across the whole piece, about −17 to −26 dB in 10-second windows, with no dramatic swells to fight the voice. The other candidates were:
- Nocturne Op. 32 No. 1, which ends stormily;
- Prelude Op. 28 No. 4, which is very short and very sad.
