# Research: "Myths of the Unbuilt" (Hugo special, working title)

A Hugo Ashby countdown of five famous myths about things that were never built. Each entry gives the myth, what the record shows, and a verdict.

Confidence: **H** means two or more solid sources agree, or the primary source was read. **M** means one good source, or sources disagree slightly. **L** means weak or disputed; L claims are never stated as fact.

**Scope decision (1 Oct 2026).** These are myths about unbuilt designs, not a list of wartime "superweapons". Nazi Bell, Nazi UFO and Philadelphia Experiment stories have no wartime sources, would amplify conspiracy content, and put advertising at risk. They stay out.

## The Sun Gun (proposed No. 1)

From the fact-check of 1 Oct 2026. The full version is in `../proposals/EVAL_sungun_tokyobay.md`.

| Claim | Finding | Conf. |
|---|---|---|
| Origin | Hermann Oberth, *Wege zur Raumschiffahrt* (1929), chapter 20. NASA translation TT F-622 (1972), pp. 479–485: https://archive.org/details/nasa_techdoc_19720008133 | H (primary) |
| What Oberth proposed | A sodium-foil mirror on a wire net, spread by rotation, 700–1,200 km up. "A reflector 100 km in diameter would, at the most, cost 3 billion Mark." About 15 years to build. | H (cost), M (years) |
| Civil purposes | Keep sea routes to Spitsbergen and Siberian ports free of ice; make northern lands "habitable"; save "fruit and vegetable crops of entire provinces" from frost. | H |
| Weapon line | "Since such a reflector could, unfortunately, also have high strategic value (munitions factories can be exploded with it, tornadoes and thunderstorms produced, marching troops and their reserves destroyed, whole cities burned…)" | H |
| His own limit | "If the reflector were 1,000 km wide, the sun's image of each facet would be 10 km in diameter." He called a novelist's one-hectare death ray impossible. | H |
| The 1945 briefing | Lt. Col. John A. Keck, US Army Ordnance, gave a press briefing in Paris at the end of June 1945. The sun gun was one item on a list. | H |
| TIME, 9 Jul 1945 | Scientists at Hillersleben planned a "space station" 5,100 miles up, with a sodium mirror of 3½ sq mi that could "make an ocean boil or… burn up a city", possible "within 50 or 100 years." Quoting the NYT: "merely dreaming over their ersatz beer." https://time.com/archive/6789822/science-sun-gun/ | H |
| LIFE, 23 Jul 1945 | Headline: "NAZI MEN OF SCIENCE SERIOUSLY PLANNED TO USE A MAN-MADE SATELLITE AS A WEAPON FOR CONQUEST." The **same issue's** sidebar, p. 80: "'SUN GUN,' PROPOSED BY ARCHIMEDES, DIDN'T WORK THEN AND IT WON'T NOW… the image of the sun… would be about 40 miles in diameter and not hot enough to do any damage." https://archive.org/details/Life-1945-07-23-Vol-19-No-4 | H (primary) |
| A real programme? | No German documents, named scientists, budget or hardware have surfaced. | H (absence of evidence) |
| Physics | The sun is 0.53° wide, so a mirror 8,200 km up throws a spot at least about 76 km across. A 3½ sq mi mirror gives about **0.2% of sunlight**. About 100 suns are needed to ignite wood, which takes a mirror about 760 km across. | H (calculated, matches LIFE and Oberth) |
| Znamya 2 | 4 Feb 1993. A 20 m mirror swept a spot about 5 km wide and about as bright as a full moon across Europe before dawn. | H |
| Znamya 2.5 | Feb 1999. It snagged on an antenna, tore, and the programme was abandoned. | H |
| Reflect Orbital | An 18 × 18 m demo mirror, about 625 km up, with a spot about 5 km wide. FCC licence 9 Jul 2026; launch targeted for late 2026. **Not confirmed launched as of 1 Oct 2026; recheck before publishing.** | M–H |
| Sensitivity | In April 1945 Hillersleben housed about 2,500 prisoners freed from the Bergen-Belsen train. Never use it as a light-hearted location. | M–H |

**Avoid:**
- "Nearly built" or "Wunderwaffe"
- "Captured blueprints"
- "150 scientists"
- "Geostationary" (that was LIFE's own illustration)
- "Oberth's 100 m mirror" (it was 100 km)
- "Oberth designed the V-2"
- A NYT date of 28 June 1945 (unverified)
- "Hitler" in the title, or a swastika in the thumbnail
- Fake LIFE pages

## Gaudí's Hotel Attraction, Manhattan (1908?) (proposed No. 3)

From the New York research of 30 Sep 2026.

| Claim | Finding | Conf. |
|---|---|---|
| The story | Two unnamed American businessmen commissioned Gaudí in May 1908 to design a giant hotel in Manhattan. | L (single source) |
| Scale | Usually 360 m (about 1,180 ft). Other versions give 320 m, or 980–1,100 ft. Every version traces back to the same single source. | M (repeated), L (origin) |
| Only source | Joan Matamala i Flotats, son of Gaudí's model-maker. He first published it in **1956**, 48 years later, and illustrated it with his own lithographic "recreations" (about 1952). Gaudí's papers mostly burned in 1936. | H that this is the chain |
| For | Joan Bassegoda (Real Cátedra Gaudí) accepted seven sketches. Marcos Mejía López's thesis (1992) found Gaudí-like catenary geometry. | M |
| Against | Luis Gueilburt (Centro de Estudios Gaudinistas): "Ni un solo dibujo original demostrable, ni una sola carta encontrada, ni una factura" ("not one provable original drawing, not one letter found, not one invoice"). Juan José Lahuerta (Càtedra Gaudí): no disciple ever mentioned it, and it was absent from the 1910 and 1927 exhibitions and the 1929 biography. No US record names the clients. | H that this is the skeptics' case |
| Site | **None was ever identified.** The World Trade Center link comes from Paul Laffoley's post-9/11 proposal. | H |
| Myth | The "client named Carlton" origin story comes from a **novel** (J.S. Raynor, *The Gaudí Façade*). | H |

Sources:
- https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hotel_Atracci%C3%B3n
- https://gaudiclub.wordpress.com/2011/02/08/el-hotel-attraction-de-new-york-un-proyecto-inventado-y-atribuido-a-gaudi/
- https://irreductible.naukas.com/2009/07/24/el-proyecto-de-hotel-gigante-de-gaudi-en-nueva-york-1908/
- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hotel_Attraction

**Verdict: unprovable.** Every image is a 1950s reconstruction, and say so on screen.
