**Case against #1 (opinion):** the question has been answered online many times, since the 2015 "Muslim woman" headlines. Our answer is better sourced, but the title doesn't say so. #4 signals the archive angle, at the cost of search traffic. **Bet:** #1 for search, then test thumbnails rather than titles.
