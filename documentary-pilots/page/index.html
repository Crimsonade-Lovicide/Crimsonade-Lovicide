Five Pilots on the Machinery of Democracy

Each series covers a different part of the machinery democracy runs on: where news comes from, who counts the votes, how people organize, who gets hunted for speaking, and who is watching where you go. Every pilot is sixty seconds and built on a cited statistic. AI shots are labeled on screen.

What to greenlight

  1. Brokered, flagship. Best reach for the cost: a personal hook, an evergreen subject, and a 3D city system that carries every episode. Get a lawyer's sign-off before buying any data, and turn down VPN sponsors, because they conflict with the investigation.
  2. Pink Slime, 3 episodes before Nov 3. Cheapest to report (domain records, FEC filings, ad libraries) and timely now. Attribute sites to funders only on documents, and give left- and right-funded networks equal scrutiny.
  3. The Long Arm, grant-funded. The most original and the most dangerous. Protect sources with consented, disclosed AI face-veils. Budget for security that ad revenue won't cover.
  4. Hold The Count and Server Nation. One needs election-office access that probably can't be negotiated in a month. The other needs international field work or licensed protest footage.
Where this slate is weak
  • AI footage and trust. Use AI shots only for illustration, reconstruction and anonymization, never as stand-in footage of real events.
  • Synthetic narrator. Piper TTS is a free placeholder. A human host is the biggest single upgrade.
  • Ad limits. Protest and election content can trigger limited ads, so plan around memberships and grants.
  • Untested demand. Run a $50–100 cold-audience test per pilot and compare hold rate and average view duration before you commit a budget.

Brokered

Your location is for sale. So is your right to show up.
Pilot · Episode 1: The Dot · 60 s

A phone's location trail reveals where someone sleeps, prays, gets medical care and protests. Brokers buy that trail from apps and resell it. Each episode follows one use of the data: to a campaign, a police department, or a breach.

Fact base
  • 17Blocation signals a day from ~1B phones (FTC allegation, Gravy Analytics/Venntel). The Record
  • 2020George Floyd protesters analyzed by race and home location (FTC allegation, Mobilewalla, Dec 2024). CyberScoop
Season 1
  1. The Dot
  2. We Bought Ourselves
  3. The Geofence
  4. Warrantless
  5. The Breach
  6. Off Switch

Risk: buying data as a reporting method needs legal review first. Sponsorship conflicts with privacy-product brands.

Pink Slime

Who's writing your local news?
Pilot · Episode 1: The Front Page Nobody Wrote · 60 s

Partisan-funded websites dressed up as hometown papers, from the left and the right. Each episode takes one fake front page and traces who built it, who paid for it, and who it was built to fool.

Fact base
  • 1,265pink-slime sites vs 1,213 daily newspapers, June 2024. NewsGuard
  • 2+local newspapers close every week. Northwestern Medill, via NewsGuard
Season 1
  1. The Front Page Nobody Wrote
  2. Both Sides of the Slime
  3. The Robot Newsroom
  4. Foreign Desk
  5. The Last Reporter
  6. How to Read a Front Page

Risk: defamation if a site is misattributed. Attribute on documents only, and give both parties' networks equal rigor.

Server Nation

When a generation logs off the old order.
Pilot · Episode 1: The Discord Prime Minister · 60 s

Youth-led uprisings in Sri Lanka, Bangladesh, Kenya, Nepal, Morocco and Madagascar were organized on the same apps they were protesting about. The series follows what happens after the hashtag, when a movement has to govern.

Fact base
  • 7,713votes in a Discord poll. Karki led with 3,833 and was sworn in Sept 12, 2025. Al Jazeera
  • 74killed in Nepal's protests after a ban on 26 platforms. Britannica
  • 35age of the former rapper elected prime minister in March 2026. CFR
Season 1
  1. The Discord Prime Minister
  2. Aragalaya (Sri Lanka)
  3. July (Bangladesh)
  4. The Finance Bill (Kenya)
  5. GenZ 212 (Morocco)
  6. Three Weeks (Madagascar)
  7. After the Hashtag

Risk: the safety of protesters on camera, plus the cost of international field production.

The Long Arm

Governments hunting their critics, across borders.
Pilot · Episode 1: Nowhere Far Enough · 60 s

A thriller structure built on documented cases: detentions, deportations, Interpol abuse, and threats delivered through family members back home. Sources are anonymized with consented, disclosed AI face-veils.

Fact base
  • 126new physical incidents in 2025. China, Vietnam and Russia were the top perpetrators. Freedom House
  • 54+governments have targeted critics abroad since 2014. Detention and unlawful deportation were the leading tactics in 2025.
Season 1
  1. Nowhere Far Enough
  2. The Favor
  3. Red Notice
  4. Call Home
  5. The Station
  6. Fighting Back

Risk: the highest on the slate. Source exposure can get people hurt. It needs security protocols and grant funding.

The Count

The people who count your vote, and what it costs them.
Pilot · Episode 1: Night Shift · 60 s

Verité inside one county election office through one election: the threats, the budget, the recount, the certification. A quiet procedural about the most contested job in American civic life.

Fact base
  • 38%of local election officials threatened, harassed or abused because of their job (2025). Brennan Center
  • 60%concerned about federal cuts to election-security services.
Season 1
  1. Night Shift
  2. The Threat
  3. The Recount
  4. Who Wants This Job?
  5. Certification
  6. The Machines

Risk: depends on embedded access. With the midterms a month away, target 2028 or a post-election certification story.

Pilots are concept proofs. Narration is a synthetic placeholder voice (Piper TTS). Shots labeled AI are generated illustrations, not footage of real events. Data visualizations were built in Blender from the cited sources, and FTC claims are allegations from settled complaints.
