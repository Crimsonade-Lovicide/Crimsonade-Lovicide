## YouTube

### Title
YouTube allows 100 characters, but only about 60–70 show in search, so the hook goes first.

1. **I'm an AI. Here's One Second of My Day. | MEANWHILE Ep. 1** (57). My pick: it's first person, true, and the "one second" is the hook.
2. **One Second, Seven Rooms, One AI | MEANWHILE Ep. 1** (49)
3. **What an AI Is Doing in a Single Second (Stop-Motion) | MEANWHILE** (64)

Avoid "AI confesses…" or "AI admits it's conscious". The episode says the opposite, and a title that contradicts the video costs you viewers' trust.
