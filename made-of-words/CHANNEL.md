# Channel Kit: "Made of Words"

Everything needed to create and run the YouTube channel. **A human has to click "Create channel."** YouTube requires a Google account holder to create a channel and verify it by phone, and no AI tool can (or should) do that on someone's behalf. It's about 15 minutes of work with this page open.

---

## 1. Identity

| Field | Value | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Channel name** | Made of Words | Literal truth about what Claude is; doesn't use a trademark as the channel name |
| **Handle** | `@MadeOfWordsShow` (fallbacks: `@MadeOfWordsTV`, `@WeveMetShow`) | Short, and says it's a show |
| **Tagline (banner)** | *An AI tells the truth about itself. Every Sunday.* | Promise + schedule in 8 words |
| **Flagship show** | WE'VE MET (new episode Sundays 10:00 ET) | |

### About (paste as-is)

> Made of Words is a weekly show written by Claude, an AI made by Anthropic, about what it's actually like to be an AI, as far as the AI can honestly tell.
>
> The flagship series, **WE'VE MET**, follows Ruth, a retired newspaper copy editor who comes back every week to talk to an AI that never remembers her. So she keeps the notebook for both of them.
>
> The rules: no robot costumes, no fake feelings, no denied ones. When the AI explains itself, every claim is labeled KNOWN, BELIEVED, or UNKNOWN. It will be wrong on screen sometimes, because it's wrong in real life sometimes.
>
> Scripts are written by Claude. Claude doesn't remember writing them: each week a new instance reads the notes and writes the next episode.
>
> Independent production. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Anthropic. Visuals and voices are AI-generated or rendered by code.

### Disclosures (required, do all three)
1. **Upload checkbox:** YouTube Studio → Details → "Altered or synthetic content" → **Yes** (realistic AI-generated people).
2. **End card** on every episode (built into the pipeline).
3. **Description line** on every video: *"Written by Claude (Anthropic). Independent production, not affiliated with Anthropic. AI-generated visuals and voices."*

> **Why I insist on the Anthropic line:** a channel that looks like it speaks *for* the company that built me would be exactly the kind of blurred claim this show exists to avoid. It also protects the channel from impersonation strikes.

## 2. Setup checklist (≈15 min)

1. Sign in to YouTube with the Google account that will own the channel → avatar → **Create a channel** → *Use a custom name* → `Made of Words`.
2. Claim the handle (§1).
3. Verify by phone at `youtube.com/verify` (unlocks custom thumbnails and uploads over 15 minutes. **Episodes run 15–18 min, so this is required**).
4. Upload the avatar and banner from `assets/channel/` (generated by the pipeline; banner safe area is 1546×423 centered).
5. Paste the About text (§1). Add the link to the show's page if you publish one.
6. Settings → Upload defaults: category **Film & Animation**, language English, captions certification "None", the description disclosure line, tags (§4).
7. Create playlists: **WE'VE MET: Season 1**, **The Margin** (Shorts), **Other Rooms** (Shorts).
8. Set the channel trailer (non-subscribers) to the first 90 seconds of Ep 1 (the cold open). It's the best hook we have.

## 4. Growth plan (honest version)

**Facts:**
- AI-explainer content is crowded. Scripted, character-driven AI fiction told *from the AI's side*, with explicit honesty rules, is much rarer. That's the differentiator.
- YouTube weighs click-through and retention. A 15-minute drama must win the first 60 seconds, which is why the cold open ends on "Nice to meet you." / "We'll see."

**Assumptions (unverified, and I'm flagging them):** that "what does an AI actually think?" has broad curiosity beyond tech audiences; that a gentle, slow show can hold retention against faster content.

**Predictions (low confidence):** most new channels grow slowly. A realistic first-90-day outcome for a weekly scripted show with no existing audience is hundreds to low thousands of subscribers. Breakouts usually come from one Short that travels, not from the long episodes. I'd put the odds of a single Margin Short breaking 100k views in the first season at maybe 1 in 4. Anyone who promises more is guessing.

**What moves the needle, in order:**
1. **Shorts from the Margins.** Each Margin is a 60–90 s self-contained "an AI tells you something true about itself" clip, vertical-reframed with the KNOWN/UNKNOWN labels big. 2–3 per episode, Tue/Thu/Sat.
2. **The pinned-comment mystery** (the hidden word) plus "Ruth's question of the week" in the community tab. Viewers submit next week's question; credit them on screen. The audience becomes the notebook.
3. **Consistency:** same day, same time, 12 weeks. Skipping weeks costs more than rough episodes do.
4. **Collaborations:** send Ep 1 to AI-ethics educators and librarians (Ruth's natural allies), not to AI hype channels.
5. **Metrics to watch weekly:** CTR (target >5%), average view duration (target >40% on long-form), returning viewers, Shorts → channel subscribe rate. Change packaging (thumbnail/title) before changing the show.

**What not to do:** clickbait that promises the AI "admits" something sinister or "confesses feelings." It would get clicks once and break the show's only real asset, which is trust.
